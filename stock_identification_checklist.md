# Stock Identification Checklist (equity, swing trading)

**Goal:** turn a big universe into a short, ready-to-act watchlist. The decision to enter or skip comes *after* this.

```
Large liquid stocks (Nifty 50)
   ↓ 1  Build the pool            (10 names, start with 5)
   ↓ 2  Trend filter              (weekly / monthly)
   ↓ 3  Mark levels               (zones, trendlines, channels)
   ↓ 4  Location filter           (where is price now?)
   ↓ 5  Red-flag screen           (events, news, volatility)
   ↓ 6  Reward-to-risk pre-check  (is 1:3 even possible?)
WATCHLIST: Ready / Watch / Not now  →  then: enter or skip
```

---

## Step 1. Build the pool

| Rule | Pass if |
|---|---|
| Liquidity | A Nifty 50 stock (these pass NSE's impact-cost test by design) |
| Volatility | ATR(14) is roughly **1.2-3.5% of price** (too low = too slow for 1:3; too high = noisy stops) |
| Safety | Not on an exchange surveillance list; no recent wild gaps |
| Spread | At most **1-2 stocks per sector** |
| Size | Start with **5**, grow to a maximum of **10** |
| Price | Not a hard limit. Position size comes from the stop distance. |

**How to check ATR%:** on the daily chart add *Average True Range* (length 14). ATR% = ATR ÷ closing price × 100.

## Step 2. Trend filter (weekly / monthly)
- Uptrend = higher highs and higher lows. Downtrend = lower highs and lower lows. Otherwise sideways.
- **Keep uptrends.** Skip downtrends and sideways stocks for now.
- Helper: price above a rising 50-day average and above the 200-day average.

## Step 3. Mark levels
- Monthly, then weekly, then daily. Draw **zones** (use wicks and repeated touches), not single lines.
- Note levels that flipped (old resistance now support). Add trendlines or channels.
- Do this on weekends, and refresh when something changes.

## Step 4. Location filter
- Shortlist only: **just above support in an uptrend**, or a **tight base just under resistance**.
- Mid-range, stretched, or freshly gapped up = wait.

## Step 5. Red-flag screen
- Results or ex-dividend date inside your holding period
- Fresh news, leadership change, regulatory issue
- Wild candles or big gaps recently
- Weak relative strength (falls more than the Nifty)
- Market at a range edge: smaller size

## Step 6. Reward-to-risk pre-check
- Stop below support, target at the next resistance. Reward must be at least **3× risk**.
- If not, don't delete the stock. Set an alert at the price where the math works.

## Routine
- **Weekend (60-90 min):** refresh trend and levels for the pool.
- **Each evening (15-20 min):** rerun steps 4-6, update alerts.
- **Morning (5 min):** check alerts, news, and any results or ex-dividend dates.

*Educational, not financial advice. Levels and dates are illustrations: verify on your own charts.*
